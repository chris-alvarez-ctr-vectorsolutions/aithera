Vector navigation shell · 7 versions

One shell, real products: every version wraps the actual Vector suite (Vector LMS · Vector EHS Management · Vector Scheduling + a cross-product Dashboard). V1 is the reference; V2a/V2b explore Vector LMS Learner/Admin sub-products, switched from the top-bar brand (V2a) vs a segmented control in the side nav (V2b), with two filter treatments on the real Training Plan; V3 is the L-shaped shell (full-height app rail, top bar inset to its right, search in the side nav); V4 to V6 vary only how the side nav shows hierarchy, with V4 in a Jira-like closed-by-default mode. V3 and V4 carry the shared interaction frames (collapsed / peek overlay / pinned). The dark review pill switches versions and toggles the customer logo, location picker and tabs. Earlier explorations live in archive/.

Fixed in every version: the shared shell contract
Nav open/close App switcher Product · Location Scoped search Alerts Help Avatar
The side nav closes two ways in every version: a control in the nav's own top-right corner, plus the top-bar toggle (V3 moves that toggle onto the side-nav column itself, under its full-height rail). The app switcher owns the top-left corner in every product, and its cross-product Dashboard entry now carries its own tiles glyph so it never reads as a product Home. Switching ≠ navigating: other products never appear inside the current product's nav tree, and universal actions never move. Only the middle (mode tabs or nothing) and the side-nav pattern vary per version.
Menus borrow two things from Convergence + EHS (the visual style stays this exploration's own): the disclosure chevron trails the label and flips when open, as in .sn-row / .sb-item, so an expandable row and a plain row at the same level share one icon x and one label x; and the menu vocabulary echoes the real products' IA: Organization / Teams / Departments, Curricula, Qualifications, Activities, My recents / Scheduled reports on the Learn side; Incidents summary, Investigations, Corrective actions, OSHA logs, and the Safety Management long tail (Hazards, JSA, SDS, Tasks, Events, Document library, Observations) on the Comply side.

V1 · Grid launcher · global scoped search

Switcher: waffle launcher + Dashboard Search: top bar · All / product scope Tabs: in top bar

The reference shell: waffle launcher, location tree picker with a level subtitle, top-bar scoped search, in-bar mode tabs, 3-level accordion nav. The nav closes from its own top-right control or the top-bar toggle, collapsing to a thin icon-only panel, and the profile menu sets side-nav text size (compact / default / comfortable). Review pill toggles logo, location and tabs.

V2a · Learner/Admin sub-products · filter panel

Sub-products: brand switcher in the top bar Tabs: none Filters: right panel

Vector LMS splits into Learner and Admin experiences, switched from the product brand in the top bar (the brand shows the active experience as its subtitle). Learner holds Home + Training plan; Admin holds the full admin tree including Reporting. No top tabs, so the Convergence-style training plan carries its filters in a persistent right panel (status, type, due, with counts).

V2b · Learner/Admin sub-products · filter dropdowns

Sub-products: segmented switch in the side nav Tabs: none Filters: dropdown chips

Same two experiences as V2a, but the Learning/Admin switch lives IN the side nav: a segmented control above the section list reconfigures the nav below it, and the brand stays plain identity. Filters are dropdown chips above the table (Status / Type / Due / Sort, with an active-filter state and Clear all). Compare with V2a to weigh both the switch placement and always-visible filters against a full-width plan.

V3 · App rail · L-shaped shell

Switcher: full-height app rail · Dashboard on top Top bar: inset right of the rail Search: in the side nav · product-scoped Logo: sticky at the nav's bottom

The rail is dominant: full height from the very top, with the top bar starting to its right (an L-shaped shell). The nav toggle tops the side-nav column itself, search lives in the nav (product-scoped, live-filters the menu), and the customer logo docks to the nav's bottom in a sticky footer zone. Closing the side nav keeps the rail; the rail's own minimal state (launcher + current product) gives the "both closed" frame.

V4 · Hierarchy: flyout panels

Hierarchy: children open to the right Jira-like: closed by default · click-away Everything else = V1

V1's shell, but a group's children open in a flyout panel to the right of the side nav; level 3 nests inside the panel. Where this lands

V1 vs V3 decides the switcher, search placement, and now the shell's shape (single top bar vs L-shaped full-height rail). V2a vs V2b decides where the Learner/Admin switch lives (top-bar brand vs a segmented control in the side nav) and which filter treatment the Training Plan gets (persistent right panel vs dropdown chips). V4 to V6 pick the side-nav depth language on an identical shell. Shell-wide behaviors are shared: two ways to close the nav (in-nav control + top-bar toggle), collapse-to-icon-panel, profile-menu text sizing, location tree picker with level subtitles, and the scoped search naming its scope. V3 and V4 also carry the shared side-nav interaction frames (collapsed → hover/peek overlay → pin → close; see the state links on their cards).

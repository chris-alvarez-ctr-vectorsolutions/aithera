Vector navigation shell · 2 versions

One shell, real products: both versions wrap the actual Vector suite (Vector LMS · Vector EHS Management · Vector Scheduling + a cross-product Dashboard) and differ in one thing: how you switch product. V1 switches from a waffle launcher in a full-width top bar; V3 adds a full-height app rail beside the side nav and keeps its location picker in the nav. Everything else is shared: mode tabs that start at the content edge, a centred scoped search, a side nav that opens and closes from the top bar and peeks open on hover when collapsed, and the tenant branding switches. The dark review pill at the bottom centre flips between the two and toggles the customer logo, location picker, accent bar and tabs. The earlier, wider set lives in git history; the original six-version exploration in archive/.

The side nav opens and closes from the top bar only. Collapsed, it keeps the active section's icon lit and peeks open as an overlay when the pointer comes within 16px of it (100ms in, 500ms out), pinning from a tab on its edge; the collapsed column stays put underneath, so nothing shifts until it is pinned. The app switcher owns the top-left corner in every product, and its cross-product Dashboard entry now carries its own tiles glyph so it never reads as a product Home. Nav rows wrap to a second line rather than truncating, and the customer logo slot is sized to EHS's tenant-logo area (a 54px lockup), and it can sit at the top or the bottom of the nav. A tenant accent bar can run under the top nav in a colour picked from the profile menu. Those are customer-branding switches, so every version can show either state rather than forking into new versions. The top-bar identity is a "Vector <Product>" wordmark lockup over one shared mark, the way the suite actually brands; the mark is the official Vector Solutions artwork loaded from the repo's shared assets, and only its navy is a labelled placeholder, and the identity is the only branded surface so the structure still leads. Switching ≠ navigating: other products never appear inside the current product's nav tree, and universal actions never move. Only the middle (mode tabs or nothing) and the side-nav pattern vary per version.
Menus borrow two things from Convergence + EHS (the visual style stays this exploration's own): the disclosure chevron trails the label and flips when open, as in .sn-row / .sb-item, so an expandable row and a plain row at the same level share one icon x and one label x; and the menu vocabulary echoes the real products' IA: Organization / Teams / Departments, Curricula, Qualifications, Activities, My recents / Scheduled reports on the Learn side; Incidents summary, Investigations, Corrective actions, OSHA logs, and the Safety Management long tail (Hazards, JSA, SDS, Tasks, Events, Document library, Observations) on the Comply side.

V3 · App rail · location in the nav

The same shell with the product switcher as a persistent full-height rail beside the side nav: Dashboard pinned on top, the waffle at the bottom opening a launcher split into your platforms and other Vector platforms. The top bar runs full width above the nav and carries the only open/close control; the location picker sits at the top of the nav instead, and the customer logo docks to its bottom. Collapsing the nav keeps the rail; both closed leaves just the launcher and the current product.

Where this lands

V1 vs V3 is one decision: does the product switcher live in a launcher inside the top bar, or as a persistent full-height rail beside the side nav? Everything else is held constant so the comparison stays clean: the top bar is full width in both and carries the only open/close control, the tabs start at the content edge, the search is centred, the collapsed nav lights the active section and peeks open on hover, and the tenant branding switches are shared.

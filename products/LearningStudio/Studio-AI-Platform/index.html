Learning Activities

Derived
Modality expressions of the information points. These are not one activity rendered differently — each is a distinct activity carrying the same teaching points. Activities exist independently of any output; unplaced ones are normal.

Express the same information points in another modality. Coverage is preserved by construction — the new activity is authored against the points, not converted from an existing activity.

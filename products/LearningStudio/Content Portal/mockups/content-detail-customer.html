Fire Safety — Custom (Acme Corp)

My Content
Course 20 min Draft Fire Safety
Description

This custom course was developed for Acme Corp and covers facility-specific fire safety procedures including emergency evacuation routes, proper use of portable fire extinguishers, fire hazard identification in the manufacturing environment, and compliance with NFPA 10 guidelines. Tailored for on-site personnel at the Millbrook facility.

Lockout/Tagout Safety — Fundamentals

Vector-Provided
Course 45 min Published Industrial Safety
Description

This foundational course covers the critical procedures required for Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) compliance under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147. Learners will understand the hazardous energy sources that require control, the correct steps for implementing lockout and tagout procedures, and how to safely verify energy isolation before performing maintenance or servicing on equipment. This course is suitable for all workers who perform service or maintenance on machinery and equipment.

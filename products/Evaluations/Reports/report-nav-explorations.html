Report Navigation — Interaction Explorations
Wireframe fidelity. Three patterns, three states each: Closed / Opening / Open.
Option A
Top Drawer — Header Expansion
A trigger in the report header expands a full-width panel downward within the content column, revealing a scrollable card catalog. The sidenav is untouched. The drawer closes on selection or dismiss.

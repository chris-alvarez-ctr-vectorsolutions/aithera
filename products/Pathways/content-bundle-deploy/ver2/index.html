Choose the district

Open the list and type to filter by name or ID. The preview shows who the bundle will reach and what is already in the district, so you can confirm it is the right site before anything is created.

    Select a district to see how many students and staff it reaches, its course data, and whether a bundle is already there.

    Link the bundle to a seal

    Seal bundles are tracked in the district's seal tracker as well as in badges. Say which seal this is, and which badges award it or count toward it.

    Does this bundle track a seal or endorsement?

    Suggested from the bundle name.

    Which seal?
    How each badge relates to the seal
    Bundle itemRole in the seal

    Activities and forms inside each badge follow the badge. The seal tracker shows one row per student for this seal, fed by the badge that awards it.

    The badges will deploy without a seal link. They will not appear in the district's seal tracker.

    Match template slots to this district's courses and exams

    The template ships placeholder course names. Match each one here, once, and the form options, activities and badge requirements are all created with the district's real names. Anything you skip is not created.

    Where should the badges go?

    Applies to all 3 badges in the bundle. Today they land at the root of Manage Badges and each one is moved by hand.

    Review, then deploy

    Everything district-specific is decided here. After deploy there should be nothing to rename inside the district.

    Paste the district's course list

    One course or exam per line, in any order. Each line is matched to the closest template slot; lines that match a synced course map to it, the rest are created as new courses.

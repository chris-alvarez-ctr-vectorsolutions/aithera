Time Log Management

How will students log time?

Students can submit the total number of hours worked during a time period (e.g., bi-weekly or monthly) without specifying the exact times worked. For example, they can simply enter "40" for hours worked Jan 1st to Jan 7th. Set a Submission Schedule below so your students can submit their hours.

Students must log the hours they worked and when they worked them during the day. For example: 9AM – 5PM on January 1st, 2024.

Mobile Logging

Mobile logging allows students to track hours in real-time on their mobile device by starting a log upon arrival and stopping it when finished. The total hours are then recorded and submitted as a daily timelog.

Who approves time logs?

Select who has permission to approve time logs that the student reports.

Time logs get approved automatically.

A member of the school staff (teachers, principals, etc) must approve the time log.

A staff member or contact at the experience must approve the time log.

Submission Schedule

Submission schedules are specific date ranges during which students can log the total hours they have worked. If students will be entering daily time logs, all logs will be collected and submitted together at the end of the submission schedule. This setting can be changed later.

You may set a specific time for this experience in which students can log their hours. Otherwise the period date range will be the same as the experience duration.

Choose the start date and end date of your submission schedule. Ex: 10/9/2024 - 10/9/2025

Set up recurring submission schedules, like weekly or bi-weekly, to automatically organize time logs.

To earn the NY Seal of Civic Readiness you need 6 total points, with minimums in each area:
  • At least 2 points in Civic Knowledge
  • At least 2 points in Civic Participation
  • 6 points overall

Per New York State regulation, each criterion awards its points only when it is fully complete. Partial work is tracked on this page so you always know where you stand, but the points land all at once.

        Implementation notes for the team · not part of the product UI

        What changed, mapped to existing components

        1. StatusIcon (in CriterionRow): two states become four: empty circle, progress ring (new, echoes the summary donuts), amber pending, green check. Read-only styling: no hover, default cursor, so it stops reading as a checkbox. Small effort.
        2. CriterionRow title row: new ProgressCount pill ("3 of 4 credits", "1 of 2 earned (max 2)") + SegmentTicks for small discrete counts. Driven by the increment records that already power evidence bullets. Small-medium effort.
        3. PointsColumn: two new states: in progress ("0 pts earned · 1 pt at completion") and pending ("1 pt pending review · 0 pts earned until approved"). The all-or-nothing point rule itself is unchanged. Small effort.
        4. Row expansion: reuses the RequirementsAccordion chevron pattern. Expanded body hosts the SubRequirementList (per-part status + evidence in one line each), replacing flat evidence bullets on multi-part rows. Medium effort.
        5. HowToEarnPanel: gains a regulation microcopy slot (framed as NY State rule, not a system limitation) and the LinkedBadgeChip replacing the dead "You may also earn…" sentence. Small effort.
        6. LinkedBadgeChip + BadgeDetailPanel: the linked badge renders merged into the criterion row (one status, inline expand, back-context line). The separate badge-modal card for seal-linked badges is retired from the student view. Medium effort, mostly data join.
        7. Submit evidence: row-level button (shown only where the district allows self-report) opens the existing activity upload/attest flow and flips the row to the pending state on submit. No change to the approval workflow itself. Medium effort.
        8. SummaryDonut: thin secondary arc + flag line for in-flight points. Category-count agnostic. Small effort.
        9. DashboardSealRow (widget): striped bar segment for in-flight points. Small effort.
        10. False-affordance fix: "Can earn multiple times" is plain muted text (it was blue link-styled); everything clickable now has hover + focus affordances, and nothing read-only does.
        11. V2 · DrillDownDrawer: the stacked "Criteria for the Seal" modals collapse into one right drawer (maps to the existing criteria overlay component: title bar + X, footer Close). Opened from the category-badge chip beside each section title or from "View full details" in any linked-badge panel. Badge requirements render as compact criterion rows with the same four states as the seal page, so students never meet a second visual language. Medium-large effort.
        12. V2 · Nested badges flattened: a badge that is itself a requirement of another badge expands inline inside the drawer with a "you are here" trail (seal › category badge › nested badge) instead of opening another overlay. Depth is unlimited in data but never more than one surface deep on screen. Medium effort.
        13. V2 · Self-report at every level: the same Submit evidence action appears on the seal row, on the drawer row, and inside a nested badge, and all of them write to the same record. Submitting anywhere flips every surface showing that requirement to Pending approval at once; no more drilling three levels just to mark something done. Small effort on top of the V1 submit flow.

        Try it: open "Civic Knowledge badge" or "Civic Participation badge" next to a section title (or "View full details" inside a linked-badge chip), expand a nested badge with the chevron, and submit a score report from inside the drawer, then close it: the matching seal-page row is already flipped to Pending approval. The seal-page submit buttons update the drawer rows the same way.

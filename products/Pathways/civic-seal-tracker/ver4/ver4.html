To earn the NY Seal of Civic Readiness you need 6 total points, with minimums in each area:
  • At least 2 points in Civic Knowledge
  • At least 2 points in Civic Participation
  • 6 points overall

Per New York State regulation, each criterion awards its points only when it is fully complete. Partial work is tracked on this page so you always know where you stand, but the points land all at once.

            V4 · what changed, mapped to existing components (all additive)

            1. StatusIcon (CriterionRow): two states become four: empty circle, partial ring (echoes the summary donuts), amber pending, green check. Read-only styling (no hover, default cursor) so it stops reading as a checkbox. Small effort.
            2. CriterionRow title row: ProgressCount pill ("3 of 4 credits", "1 of 2 earned (max 2)") + SegmentTicks, driven by the increment records that already power evidence bullets. The title itself becomes a link that opens the side panel; the row stays a summary. Small-medium effort.
            3. PointsColumn: adds in-progress ("0 pts earned · 1 pt at completion") and pending ("pending review · 0 pts earned until approved") states. The all-or-nothing point rule is unchanged. Small effort.
            4. EvidenceList: unchanged component; on multi-part rows it is the itemized per-increment progress log under the count. No effort.
            5. HowToEarnPanel: same gray panel; gains the regulation microcopy slot (framed as NY State regulation) and the "also earn via badge" line becomes a chip that opens that badge in the side panel: no dead text, and no separate competing badge modal. Small effort.
            6. Side panel (new overlay; the only new component): a stacked-navigation drawer. Its content reuses the EXISTING badge-modal criteria-card pattern (name link, colored left edge, status label), upgraded from binary green/red to the three-state + pending system. Badge cards push a nested panel page; breadcrumbs at the top track the path and every crumb is clickable; nesting is arbitrarily deep. Medium-large effort, mostly composition of existing pieces.
            7. Activity cards: simple attestations submit inline on the card ("I completed this"); evidence-based ones push a form page in the panel (description, file upload, rubric download) that submits into the existing upload/attest + admin approval flow. After either, every surface showing that requirement (card, nested badge card, seal-page row) flips to Pending approval because they read one record. Medium effort.
            8. SummaryDonut + DashboardSealRow: structure unchanged; rings and bars gain a light-gold segment for in-flight points plus a legend and a small flag line ("1 pt in progress"), so partial work never displays as a flat zero. Small effort.
            9. Removed from student view: the separate badge modal for seal-linked badges (its card pattern lives on inside the panel). Non-seal badges are untouched.

            Try it: click "4 Credits of Social Studies" to open its badge in the side panel, click "12th Grade Social Studies" to push a nested page (breadcrumbs jump back to any level), then "Report this credit" for the evidence form: submitting flips the card, its parents, and the seal-page row to Pending approval. For the one-click path, open "High School Civics Project" and use "I completed this" on the 2nd-project activity card. Escape or X closes the panel.

To earn the NY Seal of Civic Readiness you need 6 total points, with minimums in each area:
  • At least 2 points in Civic Knowledge
  • At least 2 points in Civic Participation
  • 6 points overall

Per New York State regulation, each criterion awards its points only when it is fully complete. Partial work is tracked on this page so you always know where you stand, but the points land all at once.

        Implementation notes for the team · not part of the product UI

        V4 · what changed, mapped to existing components (all additive)

        1. StatusIcon (CriterionRow + category headers): two states become four: empty circle, a ring that fills with progress (echoes the summary donuts) until it becomes the green check, and an hourglass for pending. The category badges (Civic Knowledge, Civic Participation) display progress the same way, with the same ring beside the section title. Read-only styling (no hover, default cursor) so it stops reading as a checkbox. There is no separate progress bar: the ring plus the count pill carry it. Small effort.
        2. CriterionRow title row: ProgressCount pill ("3 of 4 credits", "1 of 2 earned (max 2)"), driven by the increment records that already power evidence bullets. The title is a link-colored button (blue + chevron, underline on hover) so clickability is obvious; it opens the side panel and the row stays a summary. Small-medium effort.
        3. PointsColumn: reduced to one line per row: points earned when anything is banked ("1.5 pts earned"), points available otherwise ("1 pt available"), or "pending review" while a submission is in flight. Secondary subtitles are gone; repeat limits live in the metadata line under the title and completion rules behind the ⓘ. The all-or-nothing point rule is unchanged. Small effort.
        4. SubRequirementList (multi-part rows): the itemized per-component log (per-item status dot, source tag, evidence date) renders inline on the seal page, READ-ONLY: no report actions at page level. Reporting always happens by drilling into the side panel, so the page stays a summary. Single-increment rows keep plain evidence bullets. Small-medium effort.
        5. HowToEarnPanel: same gray panel, collapsed to a one-sentence surface ("Complete 4 credits of high school social studies coursework."). The regulation microcopy (framed as NY State regulation) and the linked-badge explanation live behind an info icon (hover or keyboard focus). The old dead "You may also earn…" text is gone; the linked badge is reachable through the row's side panel, never as a competing modal. Small effort.
        6. Pending stays contained: a pending submission never restyles the whole row or card (no amber edge, no title chip). It shows only in the status icon, the points column, the status chip on the panel card, and as a "Pending approval" line in the "Completed" log. Small effort.
        7. Side panel (new overlay; the only new component): a wide stacked-navigation drawer (~800px). Its content reuses the EXISTING badge-modal criteria-card pattern, laid out as the same card GRID the modal uses (name link, colored left edge, status label), upgraded from binary green/red to the three-state + pending system. Badge cards push a nested panel page; breadcrumbs at the top track the path, every crumb is clickable, and a back arrow in the header steps up one level; nesting is arbitrarily deep. Each criterion's page lists every fulfillment option a student can self-report on (multiple qualifying exams, alternate credit paths like transfer or summer coursework, multiple project types), so drilling in always answers "what can I do next?". Medium-large effort, mostly composition of existing pieces.
        8. Activity cards: simple attestations submit inline on the card ("I completed this"); evidence-based ones push a form page in the panel (description, file upload, rubric download) that submits into the existing upload/attest + admin approval flow. After either, every surface showing that requirement (card, nested badge card, seal-page row) flips to Pending approval because they read one record. Medium effort.
        9. SummaryDonut + DashboardSealRow: structure unchanged; rings and bars gain a light-gold segment for in-flight points plus a legend and a small flag line ("1 pt in progress"), so partial work never displays as a flat zero. Small effort.
        10. Removed from student view: the separate badge modal for seal-linked badges (its card pattern lives on inside the panel). Non-seal badges are untouched.

        Try it: click "4 Credits of Social Studies" to open its badge in the side panel (the page's credit list itself is read-only), click "12th Grade Social Studies" to push a nested page (back arrow steps up, breadcrumbs jump to any level), then "Report this credit" for the evidence form: submitting updates the count to "3 of 4 · 1 pending" and adds a Pending approval line to "Completed", without restyling the row. Each criterion page offers several reportable options: try the one-click "I completed this" on the town hall simulation under "High School Civics Project". Hover the ⓘ on any how-to line for the regulation and badge detail. Escape or X closes the panel.

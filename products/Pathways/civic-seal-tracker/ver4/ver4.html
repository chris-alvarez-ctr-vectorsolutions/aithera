To earn the NY Seal of Civic Readiness you need 6 total points, with minimums in each area:
  • At least 2 points in Civic Knowledge
  • At least 2 points in Civic Participation
  • 6 points overall

Per New York State regulation, each criterion awards its points only when it is fully complete. Partial work is tracked on this page so you always know where you stand, but the points land all at once.

        V4 · what changed, mapped to existing components (all additive)

        1. StatusIcon (CriterionRow + category headers): two states become four: empty circle, a ring that fills with progress (echoes the summary donuts) until it becomes the green check, and an hourglass for pending. The category badges (Civic Knowledge, Civic Participation) display progress the same way, with the same ring beside the section title. Read-only styling (no hover, default cursor) so it stops reading as a checkbox. There is no separate progress bar: the ring plus the count pill carry it. Small effort.
        2. CriterionRow title row: ProgressCount pill ("3 of 4 credits", "1 of 2 earned (max 2)"), driven by the increment records that already power evidence bullets. The title is a link-colored button (blue + chevron, underline on hover) so clickability is obvious; it opens the side panel and the row stays a summary. Small-medium effort.
        3. PointsColumn: reduced to one line per row: points earned when anything is banked ("1.5 pts earned"), points available otherwise ("1 pt available"), or "pending review" while a submission is in flight. Secondary subtitles are gone; repeat limits live in the metadata line under the title and completion rules behind the ⓘ. The all-or-nothing point rule is unchanged. Small effort.
        4. SubRequirementList (multi-part rows): the itemized per-component log (per-item status dot, source tag, evidence date) renders inline on the seal page, READ-ONLY: no report actions at page level. Reporting always happens by drilling into the side panel, so the page stays a summary. Single-increment rows keep plain evidence bullets. Small-medium effort.
        5. HowToEarnPanel: same gray panel, collapsed to a one-sentence surface ("Complete 4 credits of high school social studies coursework."). The regulation microcopy (framed as NY State regulation) and the linked-badge explanation live behind an info icon (hover or keyboard focus). The old dead "You may also earn…" text is gone; the linked badge is reachable through the row's side panel, never as a competing modal. Small effort.
        6. Pending stays contained on the seal page: a pending submission never restyles the whole row (no amber edge, no title chip); it shows in the status icon, the points badge, and as a "Pending approval" line in the "Completed" log. Panel cards follow production behavior, where the left edge IS the status, so they flip to an amber edge with a "Pending approval" status word. Small effort.
        7. Side panel (new overlay; the only new component): a wide stacked-navigation drawer (~800px) whose pages reproduce the PRODUCTION badge detail view as-is: intro text, green rule heading, divider, "Badges:" / "Activities:" groups, and the exact card treatment (white card, thick colored left edge, centered underlined link name, centered status word, Close in the footer). The status set extends production's Complete/Incomplete with In progress, Pending approval, and Rejected. Navigation matches production exactly: a badge-name link opens a NEW panel over the current one (each layer slightly narrower so the stack is visible), activity-name links open the evidence form as another layer, and X / Close / Escape dismisses the top layer to reveal the one beneath. No breadcrumbs and no upward navigation: category context is already surface level on the seal page. Medium-large effort, mostly composition of existing pieces.
        8. Activity cards: clicking an activity's link opens the evidence form page in the panel (description, file upload, rubric download, attestation checkbox) which submits into the existing upload/attest + admin approval flow. Every surface showing that requirement (activity card, parent badge cards, seal-page row) flips to Pending approval together because they read one record. Medium effort.
        9. Rejected state: a rejected submission renders as a production card with a dark-red edge and a "Rejected" status; the reviewer's reason is delivered by email, and clicking the card dismisses it (see the Middle School Capstone Project under Civic Participation). Small effort.
        10. Points + count as one pair: the row's progress count and points value sit side by side as the same Lumo badge: muted count first, colored points second (primary blue until earned, green when earned, amber while pending), making it visually explicit that filling the count fills the points. Small effort.
        11. SummaryDonut + DashboardSealRow: structure unchanged; rings and bars gain a light-gold segment for in-flight points plus a legend and a small flag line ("1 pt in progress"), so partial work never displays as a flat zero. Small effort.
        12. Removed from student view: the separate badge modal for seal-linked badges (its card pattern lives on inside the panel). Non-seal badges are untouched.

        Try it: click "4 Credits of Social Studies" to open its badge panel (production card styling), click "12th Grade Social Studies" to open its panel over it, then the activity link for the evidence form (a third layer): submitting closes the form layer and flips every surface to Pending approval, including the count badge ("3 of 4 · 1 pending") and the "Completed" log on the page. Click "Civic Participation" for the rejected Middle School Capstone card: click it to dismiss it. Hover the ⓘ on any how-to line for the regulation and badge detail. X, Close, or Escape dismisses the top panel; clicking the backdrop closes them all.

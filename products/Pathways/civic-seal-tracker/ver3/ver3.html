To earn the NY Seal of Civic Readiness you need 6 total points, with minimums in each area:
  • At least 2 points in Civic Knowledge
  • At least 2 points in Civic Participation

Per New York State regulation, each criterion awards its points only when it is fully complete. Partial work is tracked on this page so you always know where you stand, but the points land all at once.

        Implementation notes for the team · not part of the product UI

        V3 · what changed, mapped to VPATH-15287

        1. AchievementsGrouping (FR-1, AC-1.1/1.2): seals and badges in one grouping ("Working on" / "Earned"), exactly one tile per achievement with art, progress bar, and status chip. The legacy badge tile for seal-linked badges is gone from the student view; non-seal badges (OSHA 10, Career Exploration) are unchanged and live in the same grouping. Clicking a seal tile opens the detail view.
        2. OQ-3 resolution shown: the seal-linked "badge" renders as the gamified artwork of the achievement and of each requirement category (tile emblem, category thumbnail), never as a separate trackable component. No badge chips, no badge objects anywhere in the student path. V2 in this folder shows the alternative "supplement" treatment for comparison.
        3. Native self-attestation (FR-2, AC-2.1/2.2/2.3): "Submit evidence" / "Submit a score report" actions sit directly on the criterion, no badge routing, with the pending state visible to the student. The action opens a right-side SubmitEvidencePanel (maps to the product's existing side-panel overlay), pre-scoped to what is being reported. Assumes approval ON; per A-3/OQ-2 the toggle lives outside the student view.
        4. Component progress (FR-3, AC-3.1/3.2/3.3): "3 of 4 credits" count + segment ticks, zero points until complete. Each summary donut ring has two same-width segments: solid gold for points actually earned, light gold for partial progress that is not yet a complete criterion, so 3 of 4 credits reads as visible progress even though it is technically 0 pts. A legend under the donuts (and under the tile bars, which share the same two tones) names all three segments: earned, in progress · not yet awarded, remaining.
        5. HowToEarnPanel removed: the gray "How to earn" panels are gone. Each criterion title row carries an info hover (ⓘ) with the how-to text; on 4 Credits of Social Studies the hover holds the NY regulation note (points awarded only when all 4 credits are complete) per AC-3.2. Rows are leaner; guidance is one hover away.
        6. Progress states (FR-4, AC-4.1): not started / in progress / pending / complete at seal, criterion, and component level; any recorded progress always displays as in progress, never a flat zero.
        7. Incremental component entry (FR-5, AC-5.1/5.2): each credit row (sized up so the action reads as a full button) carries its own "Report this credit" action; the side panel opens pre-scoped to that component, and a submission updates the k of N display without completing the criterion, through the same approval flow.
        8. Criterion and component drill-down: every criterion title (and each credit inside 4 Credits of Social Studies) is a link that opens its detail in the side panel, matching the product's current drill-down depth (criterion → component). One panel shell with a stack: back arrow walks up a level, X closes everything, and the Report action is available at every level, including inside a component's own panel. Submitting steps back to the detail panel with the pending state already applied, because every surface reads the same record.
        9. Source labels + D-1: components show "From transcript" vs "Self-reported"; transcript-fed components auto-track once VPATH-15288 lands, self-reported ones work out of the box today.
        10. Chassis: categories, donuts, and tiles all render from seal config; nothing is hardcoded to two categories or six seals (Indiana's three columns and Florida's best-combination fit the same components).

        Try it: start on My achievements, open NY Seal of Civic Readiness, hover the ⓘ on a criterion title for its how-to and regulation text, then click "4 Credits of Social Studies" to open its detail panel, click a credit to go one level deeper (back arrow returns), and use Report at any level: submitting steps back with the pending state applied everywhere, including the page behind the panel. Escape or X closes the panel stack.

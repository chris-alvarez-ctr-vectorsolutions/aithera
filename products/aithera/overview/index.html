Learning Engine: Bloodborne Pathogens

Bloodborne Pathogens (RVCT-303B) rebuilt on the Learning Engine. A short pre-check decides what each learner can skip or needs more of, the content changes by workplace sector, and every learner finishes on a live scenario. Module 4, Contain the Sharp, is the fully built module.

Explorations: "Do" activities

Seven low-cost ways for a learner to show they can apply what a module taught, priced between no AI calls and the full Scenario Simulator. The sharps wording is stand-in copy.

  • "Do" activity options ExplorationOct 6

    The seven activities compared by AI cost, authoring effort and strength of evidence, with the guardrails for anything that calls a model.

  • 1: Fix the message ExplorationOct 6

    Swap the problem phrases in a draft message to a coworker, then see how they reply.

  • 2: Write it up ExplorationOct 6

    Write the incident report. Conclusions stated as fact get flagged in your own words.

  • 3: Ask three ExplorationOct 6

    Ask a coworker three questions after a near-miss. The questions you choose are the evidence.

  • 4: Defend your call ExplorationOct 6

    Pick what to fix first, then say why in one sentence.

  • 5: Spot the flaw ExplorationOct 6

    Find the one wrong sentence in a plausible explanation and say what it should say.

  • 6: Fresh case ExplorationOct 6

    The AI writes a different situation for each learner; fixed rules do the grading.

  • 7: Probe the miss ExplorationOct 6

    A rules-only check, then one question aimed at the learner's specific mistake.

Lesson interaction experiments

    All the experiments below as cards, with longer descriptions.

  • Video reflection A ExplorationSep 10

    After a training video, the learner reflects in a short written conversation with an AI, which unlocks Continue once the answer is thorough enough.

  • Video reflection B ExplorationSep 10

    The same reflection shown two ways: a pop-up that pauses the video, or a small assistant beside the still-playing video.

  • Terms to remember ExplorationSep 10

    A key term and its definition on a flip card, with an AI assistant for going deeper.

  • Quick question ExplorationSep 10

    One question at a time with large answer choices and an optional "tell me more".

  • Peer results ExplorationSep 10

    The learner answers a couple of questions, then sees how they compare with their peers on a simple chart.

  • Conversation step-in ExplorationSep 10

    The learner reads a realistic text conversation, then takes it over partway through while a peer responds in character.

  • Matrix card sort ExplorationSep 10

    A pre-course survey where items are sorted by importance instead of rated in a grid, compared with the grid it replaces.

  • Audio summary ExplorationSep 10

    Replaces a narrated slide video: the device reads the on-screen text aloud and highlights each word, so updating narration means editing text.

  • Confidence check ExplorationSep 10

    An AI coach asks the learner to rate their confidence, reacts, and asks two short follow-up questions before Continue unlocks.

  • Personalized FAQ ExplorationSep 10

    An FAQ rewritten around the learner's school and state, which asks for their guess before showing each answer.

  • Blood alcohol estimate ExplorationSep 10

    The learner guesses their blood alcohol level, then a live calculator reveals the real number beside their guess.

  • Voice input for the coach ExplorationAug 24

    A microphone button in the coach's text box: dictate, review the text, then send.

  • Direct practice chat ExplorationSep 10

    An early practice chat with an AI coach, on intervening for a friend.

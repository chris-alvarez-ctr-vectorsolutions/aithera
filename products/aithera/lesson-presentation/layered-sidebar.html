Anatomy of a hard conversation

Almost every difficult conversation moves through the same three beats. Learn the shape and you'll never feel lost mid-conversation.

  1. 1

    Open with the facts

    Name what happened plainly, without blame or story. "The report went out a day late." — not "You dropped the ball again."

  2. 2

    Explore their side

    Ask a genuine question and actually listen. You're gathering their view before you push your own.

  3. 3

    Agree a next step

    Close on something concrete you both own — so the conversation changes what happens next, not just how the moment felt.

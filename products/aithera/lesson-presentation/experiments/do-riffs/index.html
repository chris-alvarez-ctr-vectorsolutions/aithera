Do Riffs

Standalone prototypes of the seven Do activities from the options page. Not wired into any module, so nothing here can touch the shipped experience. Riffs badged "Live model call" reach the deployed worker (Haiku 4.5) and fall back to a scripted stand-in when they can't. Add ?offline=1 to any riff to force the stand-in and spend nothing.

← Back to the options page

  • 01No model calls
    Fix the message
    Swap the problem phrases in a draft chat message, then see how the coworker replies.
  • 02Live · 1 call
    Write it up
    Write the incident report from what was reported; their own words get flagged where a conclusion is stated as fact.
  • 03Live · 3 tiny calls
    Ask three
    Three questions to the coworker after a near-miss. The questions asked are the evidence.
  • 04Live · 1 call
    Defend your call
    Pick what to fix first, then say why in one sentence. Separates a good guess from understanding.
  • 05Live · 1 call
    Spot the flaw
    Find the one wrong sentence in a plausible explanation, then say what it should say.
  • 06Live · 1 call
    Fresh case
    The model writes a different situation for each learner; rules do all the grading.
  • 07Live · 1 call
    Probe the miss
    A rules-only check, then one question aimed at the learner's specific mistake.

Sharps content throughout is stand-in copy written for these experiments, not deck wording.

    Result

    Designer notes (not learner copy).

    Tests: whether the learner can tell nearly-right practice from right practice, and can name the hazard and a correct replacement in their own words. Step 1 is rule-graded (2 taps allowed); step 2 carries the real evidence.

    Cost: exactly 1 Haiku call per attempt, on Submit only (~150 input and under 250 output tokens). Blank or unsure answers make no call. Use ?offline=1 to force the keyword stand-in.

    Scripted vs live: the three explanations, the tap grading, the corrected sentences and the hazard notes are all authored. Only the judgement of the typed answer is live.

    A real build would still need: reviewed content from the subject-matter owner (this wording is stand-in, based on standard practice, not course copy), more than three flaws per topic so retries stay fresh, a stronger fallback than keyword overlap, and result logging.

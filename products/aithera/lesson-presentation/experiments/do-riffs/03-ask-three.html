      Designer notes

      Tests: whether the learner knows which questions matter after a sharps near-miss (container fullness, reach, interruption, reporting). The questions they choose are the evidence. The stand-in scenario and answers are placeholders based on standard practice, not official course copy.

      Cost: up to 3 tiny live calls (Haiku, JSON-only, max 60 output tokens each, input is only the answer list plus the one question). The closing pick is rule-graded: zero calls. Add ?offline=1 to force the keyword matcher with zero calls.

      Scripted vs live: every coworker answer, the findings board and the closing grade are scripted. The model only returns a key from the authored list and never writes or grades anything.

      A real build would still need: a content-reviewed answer bank per scenario (with the sentence that each fact supports), a wider test set of learner phrasings to tune the matcher, a decision on how many repeats or off-topic questions are free, and a tested spoken or typed accessibility path.

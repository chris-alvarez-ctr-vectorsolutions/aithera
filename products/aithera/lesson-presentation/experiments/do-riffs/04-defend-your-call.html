    Designer notes

    What it tests: whether a correct (or incorrect) pick was reasoned. The card pick is graded by rules; the one sentence checks the reasoning is consistent with the pick, not whether the pick was best.

    Cost: 1 Haiku call per run, at most 250 output tokens, plus a short prompt. Zero calls for a blank, "I don't know" or very short answer. ?offline=1 forces the stand-in.

    Scripted vs live: scene, ranking, reasons and all result copy are scripted. Only the supports yes / partly / no judgement is live. Offline stand-in counts keyword groups that match the authored reasons (2+ = yes, 1 = partly, 0 = no).

    A real build still needs: the module's real scene and ranking from the content team (everything here is stand-in practice, not official copy), a reviewed set of reasons per card, tuning of the fallback vocabulary, and a decision on whether a "no" ever affects completion (here it never does).

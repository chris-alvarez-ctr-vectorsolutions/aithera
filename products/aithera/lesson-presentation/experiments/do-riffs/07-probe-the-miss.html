      Designer notes

      Tests: whether a learner can repair their own specific slip when it is pointed at, not just re-read the right answer. The pick-3 activity is a stand-in host; the add-on is the part being tried.

      Cost: one small call per run with a slip (grading the answer, capped at 150 output tokens on the fast model), and zero for a perfect run. A blank, "idk", or question-shaped answer is handled with no call. Add ?live-question=1 to also have the model write the question (a second call): in testing, 9 of 9 live questions named the answer or implied a step order, so the code rejects those and falls back to the authored question.

      Scripted vs live: picks, the right set, the slip priority, the facts and the questions are all authored in code. Live: only the yes/partly/no verdict plus note (given just the authored fact). Any failure falls back to keyword grading. Add ?offline=1 to force that.

      Content: stand-in practice for this riff, not official course copy. A real build would still need: content-owner sign-off on the right set and facts, a check that generated questions never leak the answer, more than one slip handled per run, logging of verdicts, and review of the live grader against real learner answers.

    Scripted: the draft, every swap, the three replies and each explanation. Live: nothing. The reply depends on the total quality of the swaps (three tiers), never on the exact combination, so authoring stays at 5 phrases x 4 options + 3 replies.

    A real build would still need: reviewed wording for the course, localisation, a decision on whether weak phrasing is flagged before sending, and a way to log swaps for reporting. Stand-in content based on common sharps practice, not official course copy.

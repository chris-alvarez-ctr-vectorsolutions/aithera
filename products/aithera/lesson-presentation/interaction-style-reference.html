What adopting the Vector theme would look like across our scenario interactions

Every interaction below is built once. The toggle up top swaps the entire page between today's tokens (lifted verbatim from scenario-live.html) and the official Vector tokens from the design docs — the same styles.js bundle production uses. Only colors, fonts, shape and elevation change; structure and behavior are held constant, because we're evaluating a re-skin, not new patterns.

The biggest visible shift is the accent: the coach world moves from indigo #4b63e6 to Vector blue #0271ce (--lumo-primary-color), and controls tighten to Vector's 4px corners. The scene's violet character maps to Vivid Violet — but its warm “you” voice stays amber: Vector's palette has no gold (its warm ramp, Ecstacy, is orange), so that's the one accent with no clean token to adopt. Flip the toggle to feel the difference.

29 minagainst a budget of eleven

Option B full is “roughly eleven minutes” in the Prototype Plan; the authoring spec says nine. Test-out removes five. The overage is almost exactly the two objectives with no stakeholder source: Why It Is Dangerous and When You Are Behind.

Feel runs first, not Know

The module opens with One Decision, an F1 Feel beat, on the spec's consequence-before-rule rule. The deck and the original sketch both lead with Know. This ordering is our call and is not written down anywhere they signed.

Only two steps actually branch

The pre-test drops The Procedure and Deferred Disposal on a clean K1 sweep, and swaps Case 2's options on test-up. Everything else runs on every path, so the three-lane picture collapses to one segment.

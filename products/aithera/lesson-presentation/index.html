Lesson Presentation Experiments

Prototypes exploring new ways to present lesson content within a shared lesson template (fixed header, fixed footer, navigation).

  • Terms to Remember A flip card centered on a single term and its definition, with an AI helper to go deeper.
  • Quick Question A single question with large, blocky responses and an optional clarifier that opens more info in a modal.
  • Peer Results A “how do you compare?” screen: pick a couple of answers, then a results page “loads” live peer responses and drops a marker onto a distribution curve with a percentile verdict. Driven by a swappable scenario config.
  • Your Peak BAC A vertical slider for rating felt symptoms on a BAC scale, a full-screen interstitial that reflects the estimate back, then a live calculator whose result updates as you go — with the earlier guess ghosted onto the same meter.

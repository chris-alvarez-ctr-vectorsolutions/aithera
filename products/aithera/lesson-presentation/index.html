Lesson Presentation Experiments

Prototypes exploring new ways to present lesson content within a shared lesson template (fixed header, fixed footer, navigation).

  • Terms to Remember A flip card centered on a single term and its definition, with an AI helper to go deeper.
  • Quick Question A single question with large, blocky responses and an optional clarifier that opens more info in a modal.
  • Peer Results A “how do you compare?” screen: pick a couple of answers, then a results page “loads” live peer responses and drops a marker onto a distribution curve with a percentile verdict. Driven by a swappable scenario config.
  • Video Reflection A A finished-video screen hands off to an AI chat that becomes the main content: it asks the lesson question, reflects on the learner’s free-text answers, probes once for more, then decides they’re sufficient and unlocks Continue.
  • Conversation Step-In A scripted text thread the learner reads, then steps into: at the party invite the reply becomes theirs, and a peer (Jordan) answers in character — practicing how to decline gracefully without losing the friendship. Wraps up with three plain-language takeaways.
  • Video Reflection B Two presentations of the same post-video reflection gate, side by side for an internal A/B: a blocking center modal (must answer) versus a persistent glowing AI orb whose panel docks beside the still-playing video and minimizes back into it. One shared chat engine and gating; only the presentation differs.
  • Matrix Card Sort A pre-course survey that replaces the row×column rating matrix with a card sort: each reason is a card the learner drags from a center deck into one of three importance buckets (drag, tap, or keys 1·2·3). Includes a “Classic” toggle to compare against the matrix it replaces.
  • Audio Summary Replaces a static text-on-background narration video with a no-video version: the browser speaks the slide aloud and each word is subtly highlighted as it’s spoken, with a stylized, audio-reactive waveform on the playback bar. Built entirely from speech + canvas — no video file.
  • Custom Code A A vertical slider for rating felt symptoms on a BAC scale, a full-screen interstitial that reflects the estimate back, then a live calculator whose result updates as you go — with the earlier guess ghosted onto the same meter.

Scenario Player · UX exploration

Coaching progress, kept inside the coach panel

Rather than ejecting the learner out to a separate "practice mode" and back, the prompts stay in the one coach conversation — and a deliberately minimal progress strip (one hairline row) sits above it so the exchange feels structured and tracked, not endless. It names the current topic and shows where you are with dots; upcoming topics stay abstract, and a finished dot is tappable to revisit what you said. Each topic is titled in the thread by a quiet divider. It shifts nothing else in the chat.

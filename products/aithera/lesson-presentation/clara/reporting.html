Where the Knowledge Layer acted

Every recomposition is logged with its trigger and its outcome — the adjustments are part of the evidence, not a black box.

Targeted practice inserted — behavioral skills · 43 learners

Learners whose scenario evidence scored “Practice Needed” on the words-in-the-moment objective received the 2-minute “Say it out loud” rehearsal in place of the generic review. 87% moved to Good or above by the closing profile.

Tested out of the mastery review · 19 learners

Learners who demonstrated the review's three objectives inside the scenario skipped it entirely — nothing waived, everything evidenced. Average of 6 minutes of seat time redirected per learner.

Scenario contextualized to the plant floor · all learners

The Marshall scenario ran in its manufacturing-floor cast for this cohort — same signed objectives, same rubric, roles and setting the learners actually work in.

Flagged for follow-up · 35 learners

Completed the course with one or more constructs still at Practice Needed. CLARA has queued a reinforcement micro-scenario for each, timed 30 days out — see the drill-down for what's queued.

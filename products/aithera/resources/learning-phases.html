The Eight Learning Phases

A new idea for better learning starts here. These eight phases name every part of the business that idea will touch — and the order to move through them — so any team can see what it would actually take to ship it.

Completion › Mastery / Outcomes
1
Outcomes

Identifying Outcomes, Needs & Opportunities

WhyWhy should this learning exist at all?
WhatUnderstand what learning needs to be administered and formalize the institution’s desired outcomes — through discovery work like interviews and surveys.
WhoStrategy / ProductSubject-matter expertsRequesting stakeholder
2
Governance

Aligning Governance & Regulatory Constraints

WhyWho agrees this should exist — internally and externally — under what constraints, and with what authority?
WhatSecure internal sign-off and the authority to proceed, then map the external regulatory constraints the compliance guarantee rests on — pushing back where warranted and building a two-way relationship with regulators.
WhoExec sponsorCompliance / Regulatory AffairsLegal
3
Architecture

Architecting the Learning & Assessment Path

WhyWhat experience will reliably produce the intended outcomes?
WhatDesign the structure and flow that delivers the outcomes and stays compliant — assessment design, knowledge-unit mapping, lesson linking, and dynamic paths.
WhoInstructional Design / Learning ArchitectureProduct
4
Content

Building the Content & Assets

WhyWhat learning assets must be built, and how will they be maintained?
WhatProduce the assets across modalities — video, text, audio, and more — building in maintainability, with heavy leverage from automation and AI tooling.
WhoContent / Instructional DesignMedia ProductionEngineering
5
Launch

Launching, Activating & Enrolling Learners

WhyHow do learners enter the experience prepared and oriented?
WhatEstablish the cleanest path in — logins, course assignment, mass and individual email, and in-product dynamic messaging.
WhoProductCustomer Success / ImplementationMarketing
6
Experience

Delivering the Learning Experience

WhyWhat does it feel like to actively engage with the learning?
WhatDeliver the most effective, efficient, and engaging experience for each learner — driving them toward their potential and toward the institution’s outcomes.
WhoProduct / UXInstructional Design
7
Support

Monitoring, Intervening & Supporting Learners

WhyHow do we support success while action is still possible?
WhatWatch progress and trouble spots in time to act — intervening dynamically through AI and system-level decisions, or manually through a learning manager.
WhoCustomer Success / Learning ManagersData & Analytics
8
Impact

Credentialing & Proving Impact

WhyHow is the individual’s achievement recognized — and to what extent were the institution’s outcomes achieved?
WhatRecord and formally credential each learner’s achievement, then map skill demonstration back to the outcomes set in Phase 1 — closing the loop from completion to proven mastery.
WhoProduct / CredentialingData & AnalyticsOriginal stakeholder
The model is a loop. Phase 8 measures impact against the outcomes you set in Phase 1 — so proving mastery feeds the next idea, rather than ending the line.

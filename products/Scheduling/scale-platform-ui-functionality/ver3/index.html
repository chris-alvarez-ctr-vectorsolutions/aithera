End-user dashboard as the landing page

An end user signs in and sees their next shift, who they're working with and what's coming up. Big quick-action buttons sit below. Today they land on a grid of icons with no schedule information.

Try it

  • Tap Details on the next shift to see the full crew.
  • Accept the callback. It joins your upcoming shifts.
  • Tap Edit on the home screen to hide or reorder sections. Your layout is remembered.
  • Review my time card is a quick action, as in the Lovable concept.

Why

6/10End users couldn't find their own shift in a roster that shows everyone
4/10Tried a misc shift or "add event" instead
4/10Failed "Request time off". The time-off features are scattered across menus.

Builds on

  • The Lovable end-user console concept (planned for 2027)
  • Read-out recommendations: an end-user dashboard and one time-off home

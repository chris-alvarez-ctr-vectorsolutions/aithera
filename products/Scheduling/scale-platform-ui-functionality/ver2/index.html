Filtering on today's scheduler

Same scheduler, with filters people actually use. Qualifiers come first and are multi-select. Then assignment (Engine 1, Truck 1…), shift length, and open slots: show, only, or hide. Two switches turn the Employees Off & Misc section and duplicate (dual-scheduled) shifts on or off. Matching rows are highlighted. The rest fold into "+N more" inside their assignment.

View as

Admin End user

Admins start on the full schedule. End users start on My schedule.

Try it

  • Filters → qualifiers FF + PM → open slots Only.
  • Open slots Hide shows only who's working.
  • Turn off Duplicate shifts. Mia Torres's second listing on Rescue 5 goes away.
  • All assignments shown opens the same sheet at Assignment.
  • Reload. Your filters are remembered.

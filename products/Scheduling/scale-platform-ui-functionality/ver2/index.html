Advanced filtering on the scheduler

Filter the day by qualifier, person, time and assignment. "Show open slots only" and "My schedule" are one tap away in the bar under the date. Matching rows are highlighted. Everything else folds away rather than shrinking.

View as

Admin End user

Try it

  • Tap Open slots, then Filters → Qualifier PM.
  • Tap My schedule to see only your assignment.
  • In Filters → Display, switch to Compact for a denser view, or hide the dual-scheduled flag.
  • Reload the page. Your filters are still set.

Why

5/10End users couldn't find an open shift with the right qualifier and wanted a filter (5/10 admins too)
6/10End users couldn't find their own shift in a roster that shows everyone
7/10Admins were overwhelmed on first sight and hit mis-taps

Density vs. readability

Customers pushed back on bulky UI in the TargetSolutions update. So filters cut volume, and a Compact density keeps the dense view for people who want it.

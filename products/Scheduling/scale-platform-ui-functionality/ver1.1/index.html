Employees Off & Misc as a bubble

Today's scheduler, full width. Employees Off and Misc live in a bubble at the bottom right. The bubble shows how many people are off and how many slots are uncovered. Tap it and it opens halfway into a drawer. Close it and it shrinks back into the circle. Admins can drag a name onto the bubble to move that person to time off or misc.

Try it

  • Tap the bubble. Tap a person to jump to the slot they left. Close it with the chevron.
  • Drag a name with the mouse, or long-press and drag on a phone. The bubble fans out into Time off and Misc. Drop on one.
  • Pick the reason and confirm. The name becomes an open slot that says who is off, and the bubble count goes up. You can undo.
  • No drag? Tap a name for Move to time off / misc.

Compared with V1

  • No strip, so the roster starts right under the date
  • The count is always visible while you scroll
  • Drag-and-drop is admin-only. End users see the bubble read-only.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Vector Solutions (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you use the TargetSolutions mobile application (the “App”). The App is provided to you through your agency or department, which controls your account and the training records associated with it.

Information we collect

When you sign in, we receive the account details your agency has set up for you, such as your name, username and department. As you use the App we collect the training completions, assignments and credentials you record or view, along with the date and time of those actions.

We also collect device information, including device type, operating system version and a device identifier, so the App can remember your country, device type and sign-in preferences and send notifications you have opted in to.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the App, to keep your agency’s training records accurate, to send the notifications you have turned on, to secure your account, and to improve the App. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Your records are shared with your agency, which is the controller of that data. We share information with service providers who help us run the App under contractual confidentiality obligations, and when required by law.

Shared devices

On a device marked as shared, the App shows who is signed in and asks for confirmation after a period of inactivity. Face ID and saved passwords are turned off on shared devices. Sign out when you hand the device to someone else.

Your choices

You can turn notifications on or off in your device settings, and change your sign-in preferences from the login screen. To access, correct or delete your records, contact your agency administrator.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@vectorsolutions.com.

Introducing VoiceCheck

Talk through your check.
We'll fill in the steps.

Speak naturally about what you're inspecting, and VoiceCheck uses AI to transcribe your words and map them to the right checklist steps — so your hands stay on the equipment, not the screen.

  • Hands-free completion
    Record a single walkthrough and let VoiceCheck check off each step for you.
  • Understands what you mean
    Intent parsing matches plain speech to the correct step — no exact wording required.
  • You stay in control
    Every auto-filled step is highlighted for a quick review before you submit.
Help VoiceCheck get smarter

By enabling VoiceCheck, you agree that your voice recordings and their transcripts may be collected, stored, and used by Vector Solutions to train and improve this feature — so it keeps getting more accurate for you and your team over time. Recordings are handled under our Privacy Policy and AI Data & Training Terms, and you can turn VoiceCheck off at any time in Settings. Please avoid speaking sensitive personal information you don't want included.
